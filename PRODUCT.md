# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages. Album metadata is embedded in the page; product and album photos link to external services.

## Users

Visitors browsing the Lucky68 fashion catalog to explore products and open their associated product or photo pages.

## Product Purpose

Lucky68 collects the products shown across its ImgBB albums into one browsable catalog. Success means visitors can quickly find an item by brand or search and open its existing product or photo link.

## Positioning

The catalog combines 120 source albums and 1,169 photos in one English/Chinese interface, organized around brand-first browsing with product-level search and direct source links.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the page in a web browser, browse or filter by brand, search titles and photo names, expand albums to inspect images, and follow external product or ImgBB links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English and Chinese interface.
- Brand, title, photo-count, and source-order sorting; brand filter and search.
- Expandable album photo grids and external product/source links.
- Static GitHub Pages delivery with no server runtime.
- Photos are hosted by ImgBB and require network access; brand logos are local SVG assets.
- This page is a catalog and link directory; it has no checkout or order flow.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Lucky68.
- Interface language can be switched between English and Chinese.
- User-requested visual references: a little of Yupoo's brand/catalog browsing, with a premium Apple-like finish and no generic AI-generated aesthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current product page: `index.html`.
- Embedded source dataset: 120 albums and 1,169 photos.
- Four local brand SVGs in `assets/logos/`.
- Existing album and product links in the embedded dataset.

## Product Principles

- Make brand browsing and product discovery direct.
- Preserve access to original albums, photos, and product links.
- Keep both interface languages available.
- Treat product imagery as the center of the catalog.
